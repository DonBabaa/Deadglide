kirky.glb — Kirky's body

The man in the fedora: Mixamo's "Boss" character (https://www.mixamo.com, Adobe), as he comes inside the clips
downloaded from there. tools/build_kirky.py stands him as he was made, sizes him, names his skeleton and his
pieces for the game and takes his cigar out; nothing else of him is changed. His hats (the shop's fedoras) are his
own hat's picture recoloured by the game (src/player/kirkyBody.js).

kirky_skater.glb — a second Kirky, kept as a spare skin (not loaded by the game)

Built by tools/build_kirky_skater.py from a skater model downloaded from Sketchfab by the project's owner (a
MakeHuman character with a skateboard). WHO MADE IT AND UNDER WHICH LICENCE STILL HAS TO BE WRITTEN HERE before
this file is used for anything: the download page has both. The script recuts his clothes, paints out the print on
his shirt and the maker's marks on his shoes, builds his hair, and adds headphones, glasses and a backpack of its own.

kirky_anims.glb, kirky_anims.json — how Kirky moves
mob_anims.json — how the monsters move

Motion capture from Mixamo (https://www.mixamo.com, Adobe): riding, pushing, walking, running, sitting, aiming,
falling, dying and the rest for Kirky; the zombies' sprint, attack, scream, climb and death, the mutant's walk, roar,
swipe, turns, jump and jump attack for the bosses, a throw, a card deal, a man going through something with both
hands (Goyitus Ultimus, reaching), and the one clip each that Hamzitus's and Rubistus's own characters came with.
Refitted to the game's own skeletons and parts by tools/build_anims.py and tools/build_mob_anims.py. One clip of Kirky's (the ollie) is from the skater model
above. The spiders' clips (walk, idle, bite, rear, die) are from a rigged tarantula downloaded by the project's owner
(dl/tarantula-animation): ITS AUTHOR AND LICENCE STILL HAVE TO BE WRITTEN HERE. The rat's run and its standing
still are from a rigged rat downloaded by the project's owner (dl/rat run, "rat_02"): ITS AUTHOR AND LICENCE STILL
HAVE TO BE WRITTEN HERE.

zombies.glb — two more zombies, and two of the bosses

Mixamo characters (https://www.mixamo.com, Adobe): a soldier in what is left of his uniform ("WorldWar zombie"), a
gaunt one bare to the waist ("Ch10"), Hamzitus Goyitus (the "parasite zombie") and Rubistus Goyitus (the "skeleton
zombie"). tools/build_zombies.py cuts each into the game's parts and puts the colours of its picture on its points.

rat.glb — the rat

A low-poly rat downloaded by the project's owner (dl/low-poly-rat, "Ratlow"): ITS AUTHOR AND LICENCE STILL HAVE TO
BE WRITTEN HERE. tools/build_rat.py cuts it into a body, a tail and four legs and puts the colours of its picture on
its points.

mobs.glb — the monsters and bosses

Built by tools/build_mobs.py. (Its Hamzitus, its Rubistus and its rat are no longer the ones drawn: see zombies.glb
and rat.glb.) The zombie, the ghost, Hamzitus and Babitus are MakeHuman / MPFB characters
(http://www.makehumancommunity.org: body, skin, eyes, teeth, "male_casualsuit02", "male_elegantsuit01", "shoes02":
MakeHuman system assets — CC0). The rat, the spiders, the skeleton, the bone-thrower and Rubistus are modelled by
the script itself.

alien.glb — the body of the three alien costumes

Built by tools/build_alien.py from the MakeHuman base mesh (CC0), reshaped by the script.
