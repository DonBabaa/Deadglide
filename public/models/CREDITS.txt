The shoes (CC-BY) were adapted for the game: thinned out, their texture resized and repainted black on white
rubber, with a white stripe added down each side. The CC0 pieces were reshaped and recoloured too (the shirt cut
longer and looser, the trousers cut wide with turn-ups and dyed khaki, the hair shortened and darkened).
The print on the shirt is the game's own, drawn in src/player/kirkyBody.js.
